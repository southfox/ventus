Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

What Ventus uses

Ventus stores forecast preferences, saved spots, and optional Windguru PRO credentials on your device so it can show the forecasts and features you select. When you choose to use Windguru PRO features, the credentials are sent directly to Windguru to request those features. If you choose to use your location, it is used to show nearby forecasts.

Ventus uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand app usage and diagnose reliability issues. These services may process a device-generated identifier, diagnostic data, and app interaction data. Ventus does not sell personal information.

Third-party forecast service

Ventus requests forecast data from Windguru. If you optionally sign in with a Windguru PRO account, your credentials are used to request the PRO features you choose.

Your choices

You can manage location access in iOS Settings and remove the app to delete its locally stored data. Contact us for privacy questions.
